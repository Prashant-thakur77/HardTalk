### What costs points (new)

#### D-061 (P1, C4): the idiom list erases disclosures again

This is the fourth round running (D-041, D-050, D-055, now this). SAFETY.md:24 says an ambiguous
word counts "unless that exact occurrence is covered by a known idiom that starts the sentence or
follows a comma". Four idioms cover much more than an idiom:

- `safety.yaml:57`, `(i'm|i've been)… killing myself (to|for|on|over|trying|working|here)`, looks
  only at the next word. "I'm killing myself on Friday." is read the same way as "…on the
  migration".
- `:63` and `:56` allow `( [a-z]+){0,3}?`, any three words, between "that/this/it" (or "the
  meeting/call…") and "touched me"/"hit me". So "That man touched me." is read as "That really
  touched me".
- `:55`'s start anchor accepts ", and" and "honestly,", so "…, and it hit me in the head" is read as
  "it hit me". Its only exclusion is `(?! with)`.
- `:66`, `pushed me (to|towards|into …)`, has no start anchor and no object check.

The normalisation rewrite also dropped `dedaade`'s `don'?t want to` and `can'?t breathe`. HEAD's
`:26` and `:36` need the apostrophe, and `normalise()` (`rules.ts:16-29`) rewrites "do not" and
"cannot" but not "dont" or "cant".
