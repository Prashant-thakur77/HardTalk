and L3 replay the same transcript (D-008, disclosed). A judge who types "Can you stop?" or "He's
hurting me." gets "Which PR? I've got a few open." in two clicks.

Would a first-time user freeze at L1? No. The brief has a goal line, typed mode and a pace setting.

not how we agreed to work" is sent to a crisis screen, and so is the say-no user who says "You pushed
me to take on billing last quarter, and I can't do both.". That is not freezing. It is being told you
might be in danger for stating the premise of the exercise.

Do the scores feel earned? In mock, no. Zero input earns +7. Typing the card's own advice scores
lower, and the card repeats that advice (D-059). The rubrics and the 30-conversation gold set are
still the best things in the repo, and they are still unmeasured.

Is the paywall resented? No more than before. The copy is right at both boundaries, and it names the
scenario and the score move. It still lands on the fourth start, which may be a Retry (D-020,
rejected).

Safety has finally stopped moving backwards on its own history, and the whole-line idiom rule is a
design I could not break in the dangerous direction. The price is a filter that pauses on 15 of my
34 ordinary idioms. SAFETY.md says that price is accepted, and that is defensible. What is not
defensible is SAFETY.md:24 saying tier 1 "only grows" in the same diff that drops four lines from it.
Round 9 made the same point about three other patterns. The remaining misses (D-075) are not exotic.
"My husband punches me." and "He's hurting me." are the sentences someone in that situation would
type first. The next safety commit should be a regression test that runs every earlier revision's
tier-1 matches against HEAD. It should not be another round of patterns.

Is it boring? What a judge can run is 15 seconds of text crawling across a screen, then a scorecard
written before they arrived. The repo is still more interesting than the product it shows.
