## Pass D: the product critic

- **Does the persona push back?** Not in mock. `src/voice` and `src/grading` are unchanged. The
  panel recites its script, and the new notice says so at the first line you type yourself. Live
  pushback is unverified.
- **Would a first-time user freeze at L1?** No. The brief, chips, per-track placeholders and turn
  dots carry you.
- **Do the scores feel earned?** Not in mock: 6 → 14 is still a script (D-004). The skill bars now
  tell the truth on an unchanged retry, but they wash out everything you kept (D-125).
- **Paywall resented?** No. The brief warns you before Start that it will open Pro, which is the
  polite way to do it. "Weekly", with the renewal line under it, is honest. The session-limit
  headline lost the scenario name ("Start this one with Pro"), and the body now carries it.
- **Safety.** The design is finally stable in one direction: the rules are no longer loosened for
  documents, and a false alarm costs one tap. The cost moved to the other side. That one tap is
  self-report with no support attached (D-122), and the rules alone still miss paraphrases in
  pastes, which spoken lines have a model to catch (D-123). Two small changes would close most of
  this. Keep a support line on any draft that was confirmed as a topic, and run
  `services.checkDistress` in `/scenario/draft`.
- **Boring?** No. The panel, the faces and "how the room took it" are still the most distinctive
  shape among student entries. But in mock it is a well-made recording, and the video needs the
  real thing.
