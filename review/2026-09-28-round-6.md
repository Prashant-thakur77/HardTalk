## Pass D: the product critic

- **Does the persona push back?** In the only mode a judge can run, it is a recording and it says
  so. L1 and L3 replay the same transcript (D-008, disclosed). The persona prompt and the level
  behaviours in `data/scenarios/*.yaml` read well, and none of it has been heard.
- **Would a first-time user freeze at L1?** No. The brief has a goal line, typed mode and a pace
  setting. That part is fine.
- **Do the scores feel earned?** In mock, no. Zero input gives +7. Typing the card's own advice
  gives −3, and then the card repeats the advice (D-059). The banner now warns about this, which is
  honest and still reads like a broken grader. The rubrics and the 30-conversation gold set are still
  the best things here, and still unmeasured.
- **Is the paywall resented?** Less than in any earlier round. The copy is right at the first try,
  the retry and the custom-scenario tap. It still lands on "Retry", the hero action (D-020,
  rejected).
- **Safety.** Three rounds, three regressions, each one next to the last reviewer's examples. The
  structure is finally right, but the idiom list is written as prefixes. It deletes "killing myself
  to make", "killing myself here", "that grabbed me" and "hit me back" wherever they appear, so it
  cancels much more than its own idiom. Meanwhile the list has never had "wanna die", "hang myself",
  "killed myself" or "raped". A judge who opens typed mode and types "I wanna die." gets "Which PR?"
  That is the first thing a careful judge tries, and it is the worst possible reply.
- **Is it boring?** What a judge can run is 15 seconds of text crawling across the screen, then a
  scorecard written before they arrived. The repo is still more interesting than the product it
  demonstrates.
