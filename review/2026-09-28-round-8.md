## Pass D: the product critic

Does the persona push back? In the only mode a judge can run, it is a recording, and it says so.
L1 and L3 replay the same transcript (D-008, disclosed). A persona that answers "He tried to rape
me." with "Which PR? I've got a few open." is not pushing back. It is not listening. That is the
mock's fault, not the design's (D-049). But a judge types a line to see what happens, and D-066
decides what they see.

Would a first-time user freeze at L1? No. The brief has a goal line, typed mode and a pace
setting.

Do the scores feel earned? In mock, no. Zero input earns +7. Typing the card's own advice scores
lower, and the card then repeats that advice (D-059). The rubrics and the 30-conversation gold set
are still the best things in the repo, and still unmeasured.

Is the paywall resented? No more than before. The copy is right at both boundaries. It lands on
the fourth start, which may be a Retry, the hero action (D-020, rejected). D-069 adds a small
unkindness: a conversation that ended on the crisis screen can be the one that uses up the last
free session.

Safety is still where every round's effort goes, and it still moves sideways. This round the
builder did exactly what round 7 asked for the lines round 7 named. Round 7 also asked that no
idiom be "a subject, a wildcard or a next-word test". The new idioms dropped the subject check
entirely, and the tier-1 edits bought fewer false positives by giving up rape, beatings and
harassment phrasings that already worked. At the same time 30 of 30 ordinary idioms still pause
the practice. The filter is now worse on my lines in both directions than it was at `2824c38`, and
better on round 7's. That is what fitting to the last reviewer's lines looks like. The fix is
method, not more lines: generate the tests from templates (subject × verb × object × tail), and let
the model layer, which is the right idea, carry the paraphrases once it has run even once with a
real key.

Is it boring? What a judge can run is 16 seconds of text crawling across a screen, then a scorecard
written before they arrived. The repo is still more interesting than the product it shows.
