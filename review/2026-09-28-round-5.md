## Pass D: the product critic

- **Does the persona push back?** In the only mode a judge can run, it does not listen. It now says
  so on screen, which is honest but still dull. L1 and L3 replay the identical transcript (Playwright:
  `identical_L1_L3: True`, D-008), which the brief now discloses.
- **Would a first-time user freeze at L1?** No. The brief is a fair preview with a goal line, typed
  mode and a pace setting.
- **Do the scores feel earned?** In mock, no. With zero input the retry goes up by +7. If you type
  a sharper answer than the recording, it goes *down* to 4/16, and the card tells you that you made
  no request (D-054). The mock banner says "Live mode grades what you actually say", but a judge
  who follows README.md:42's invitation to rewrite the line sees a grader that looks broken. The
  rubric and the 30-conversation gold set are still the best things in the repo, and still
  unmeasured.
- **Is the paywall resented?** Less than in any earlier round. It is correct at every moment I
  reached: first try, retry, cross-level and custom scenario. It still lands on "Retry", the hero
  action (D-020, rejected).
- **Safety.** This is now the thing most likely to sink the entry with a careful judge. For two
  rounds running, the fix for the last reviewer's examples has broken lines just next to them.
  Round 4 lost "I've been cutting myself for weeks". Round 5 lost "I'm going to kill myself on
  Friday", which the previous commit caught. It also lost "Stop it.", and the crisis screen now
  opens on "The layoffs hit me too." The pattern is overfitting to the reviewer's probe list.
  Treat it as a keyword list with a documented false-positive tolerance. That means unconditional
  self-harm and assault patterns, with whole-phrase idiom exceptions and a large test table. Stop
  tuning lookaheads.
- **Is it boring?** What a judge can run is still 15 seconds of text crawling across the screen,
  then a scorecard written before they arrived. The repo is more interesting than the product it
  demonstrates.
