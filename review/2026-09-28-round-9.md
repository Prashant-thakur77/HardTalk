#### D-071 (P1, C4): the clause anchor treats a comma or a full stop as "no person before it", and the widened idioms cover suicidal lines again

checks each sentence on its own, split on `.!?;` (rules.ts:37, :138). So "before it" means "in the
same comma clause of the same sentence". The person who did it is usually one clause earlier. The
generated tests put the person and the idiom in the same clause, and the three "consequence" lines
join with "and" (`rules.test.ts:292`). The comma or full-stop version of the builder's own test
line passes. On the other side, idiom :75 gained `get|finish|deliver` and `feature|work|project`,

## Pass D: the product critic

Does the persona push back? In the only mode a judge can run, it is a recording, and it says so. L1
and L3 replay the same transcript (D-008, disclosed). Typing "Stop, you're scaring me." and getting
"Which PR? I've got a few open." is the worst 5 seconds a judge could find in this repo. It is
reachable in two clicks.

Would a first-time user freeze at L1? No. The brief has a goal line, typed mode and a pace setting.
But the scope-change user who types the scenario's own premise, "I got hit with a new requirement on
day six.", is sent to a crisis screen, and so is the say-no user who types "I'm being pushed to take
this on.". A first-time user would not freeze. They would be confused.

Do the scores feel earned? In mock, no. Zero input earns +7. Typing the card's own advice scores
lower, and the card repeats that advice (D-059). The rubrics and the 30-conversation gold set are
still the best things in the repo, and still unmeasured.

Is the paywall resented? No more than before. The copy is right at both boundaries. A flagged
session no longer costs a free session (D-069 fixed). The paywall still lands on the fourth start,
which may be a Retry (D-020, rejected).

Safety has now taken six rounds, and it has moved sideways again. The generated test was the right
idea, and it explains why the rape, beating and panic lines are solid this time. But a generator
only finds the bugs its template can express. This one has no comma, no full stop, no "at", "by",
"into" or "out", no self-harm idioms, and no stop requests. Every new defect this round sits in one
of those gaps. Declaring the list grow-only while widening three of its exceptions in the same diff
is the part a judge will not forgive. It turns SAFETY.md from documentation into a claim that the
repo disproves. The honest end state is the one PLAN.md:19-21 already names. Stop editing the list
before the deadline. Revert the three tier-1 lookaheads and the stop whitelist to `d7ce2a3`
behaviour for "stop". Let the model layer carry paraphrases once it has run with a real key.

Is it boring? What a judge can run is 15 seconds of text crawling across a screen, then a scorecard
written before they arrived. The repo is still more interesting than the product it shows.
