→ `trackGradeSchema(track.rubrics)` gives a per-track strict output schema on the server

place that turns a scenario into faces, moods and pitches. Every new UI export has a caller
(`reactionMood`, `Celebration`, `SpeakingIndicator`, `Room`, `ScoreRing`, `SkillBar`). Faces are
`accessible={false}` / `accessibilityElementsHidden`. Tabs are a `tablist` of `tab`s with
`selected` state.

## Pass D: the product critic

- **Do the personas push back?** In the recorded Interview L1, Tom answers his own question for
  the user ("Nice, that's a classic… A load test in the release checklist would have caught it."),
  and the panel folds as soon as the user gives a number. That is fine at L1. But mock mode
  replays the same script at L3, so no one here can see L3 bite.
- **Would a first-timer freeze?** The new briefs are the best thing in the round: who is in the
  room, their stance in words, and three "will ask about" chips for each person. The catch is
  that the Interview and Pitch scenarios hand the user someone else's story ("Tell the story of
  the booking app that crashed", "You just pitched Huddle"). You rehearse a fictional candidate's
  failure, not your own. Your own story is Pro-only.
- **Do scores feel earned?** In mock they are typed into YAML and follow the anchors carefully
  (for example, Evidence 1 → 4 on the interview retry, citing "400 students…" and "520 students
  booked with zero failures"). But the gate lets a 4 stand on a quote that doesn't show the
  anchor (D-087), and the nine new rubrics have never been measured against a human (D-084).
  Citing Kolb's learning cycle for "Ownership" is a stretch a sharp judge will notice.
- **Is the paywall resented?** No. It opens at the 4th graded start or a custom-scenario tap,
  names the conversation and the user's own 6 → 15, and "Not now" goes back cleanly.
- **Boring?** Less than before. Faces, a panel and four tracks make a better first 20 seconds.
  But the core loop is still unproven live, and widening it on deadline day adds untested surface.
