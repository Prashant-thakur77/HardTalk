## Pass D: the product critic

- **Does the persona push back?** Not in mock; `src/voice` and `src/grading` did not change.
  The recording plays. The off-script notice now offers a one-tap restart, which is the right fix.
- **Would a first-time user freeze at L1?** No. The brief, chips and turn dots carry you, and
  the free-session captions tell you where the paywall is before you hit it.
- **Do the scores feel earned?** Not in mock (D-004). The grey kept steps finally read honestly.
  One cost: an unchanged 15/16 retry is now an all-grey scorecard, which looks like nothing
  happened. That is accurate, but it is flat.
- **Paywall resented?** No. Three warnings before the gate (scorecard, home, brief), and the
  paywall names the scenario and the score so far. It is polite and scenario-aware, and it has
  never run against RevenueCat.
- **Safety.** The design is now in the right shape: hold, confirm with two taps, keep support on
  the practice, and ask instead of ending. But the ask is not wired into the two things that
  decide what happens next, the footer's priority order (D-128) and the server's grade gate
  (D-129). Both are small fixes. Rank `askingIfPersonal` (or a support link) above `ungradable`,
  and let `/grade` accept a flagged line on a `sensitive_topic` scenario once the user has
  answered "topic". Stop tuning regexes (D-130): in mock, say that only the rules run.
- **Boring?** No. The panels, the faces and "how the room took it" are still the most
  distinctive shape among student entries.
